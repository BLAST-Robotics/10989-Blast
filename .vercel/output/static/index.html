What We Do

Discover what makes Team 10989 Blast Robotics a competitive force in FIRST Robotics Competition and our community.

Design & Build Robots

Engineer and construct competitive robots using CAD design, machining, and advanced manufacturing techniques.

Programming & Controls

Develop autonomous and teleoperated robot control systems using Java and advanced programming techniques.

Compete Globally

Participate in regional and world championship competitions, showcasing our skills against top teams worldwide.

Community Outreach

Inspire STEM education through demonstrations, workshops, and mentoring younger students in our community.

Skill Development

Build technical, leadership, and teamwork skills through hands-on projects and collaborative problem solving.

Awards & Recognition

Earn prestigious awards and recognition for excellence in engineering, innovation, and gracious professionalism.
